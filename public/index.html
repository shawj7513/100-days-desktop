    Help

    Getting started
    1. Click the course day you're working on (the numbers on the grid).
    2. Click Start session. A timer appears at the top.
    3. When you're done, click Stop. The time is saved.
    4. Finished the whole day? Open it and click Mark complete.

    You can close the app while a session is running; the timer keeps counting from the real clock when you come back.

    Pausing, and the minimum session

    Pause for interruptions. Paused time isn't counted. Resume carries on.

    A session must have at least the minimum active time (20 minutes unless you changed it in Settings). If you click Stop too early, the session keeps running; keep going, or Discard it.

    You can do several sessions in one calendar day, even for different course days. Each session belongs to exactly one course day.

    I forgot to press Start

    Open the day and use Add a past session. Enter when you started and stopped, and how many minutes you paused. A past session can't overlap another one or end in the future.

    What the stats mean
    • Hours logged: all active time in saved sessions.
    • Real days per course day: days since your start date divided by course days completed. It's your pace.
    • Projected finish: today plus (course days left × your pace). It moves as your pace changes.
    • Current streak: calendar days in a row with at least the minimum minutes. If you haven't studied yet today, yesterday's streak still shows.
    • Longest streak: your best run so far.
    • Most time-consuming day: the course day you've spent the most minutes on.
    Where is my data? Backups and moving PCs

    Everything lives in one file, . Nothing is sent over the internet.

    Back up: Settings → Back up my data, or just copy that file.

    Restore or move to a new PC: close the app, then put the backup file in the data folder named tracker.db (replace the old one).

    Uninstalling the app doesn't delete your data folder.
